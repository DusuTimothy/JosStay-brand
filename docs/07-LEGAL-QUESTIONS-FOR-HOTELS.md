# JosStay — Legal Questions to Ask a Hotel

**Status:** Meeting aid, **not legal advice**. A Nigerian lawyer must review the answers and draft the final agreement.
**Date:** 17 November 2026
**Use with:** `08-NON-LEGAL-QUESTIONS-FOR-HOTELS.md`, `09-MONEY-QUESTIONS-FOR-HOTELS.md`, `10-POLICY-QUESTIONS-FOR-HOTELS.md`
**Backing docs:** `04-LEGAL-AND-POLICIES.md`, `05-PRODUCTION-CHECKLIST.md`

---

## How to use this in the meeting

Ask in this order. Write the **actual answer**, not "yes". Ask to see the document, not just hear the claim. Record owner and manager names in an internal file — **never on the public website**. Do not promise anything not in the approved docs. If an answer is evasive, stop and come back with counsel.

> Reminder of the model: the hotel keeps its name, prices and rules. JosStay verifies the **listing**, brings guests and takes **10%** commission. We do not guarantee hotel service.

## 1. Identity and authority to sign

| # | Ask | Why it matters | Good answer / red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "What is the exact registered legal name of the business, and what trading name does the hotel use?" | The contract must be with the real entity. The public page shows the hotel's name, not an owner's. | Good: gives both names and can show the CAC document. Red flag: "we never registered it". |
| 2 | "Who has authority to sign the partner agreement? What is your title?" | A signature from someone without authority is worthless later. | Good: director, owner or manager with a signed authority letter. Red flag: "I can ask my brother". |
| 3 | "Is there a co-owner, shareholder or management company whose approval is needed?" | Avoids a later ownership dispute over listing or money. | Good: named, documented. Red flag: shifting answers. |
| 4 | "Do you manage this property for someone else?" | You need the principal's written consent, not just the manager's. | Good: management agreement shown. Red flag: vague arrangement. |
| 5 | "Is there any ownership or partnership dispute right now?" | A disputed hotel cannot safely be published or paid. | Good: clear "no" in writing. Red flag: hesitation, "almost settled". |

## 2. Registration and tax identity

| # | Ask | Why it matters | Good answer / red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | "What is your CAC registration number? Can I see the certificate?" | Confirms a real business; Paystack KYC needs it. | Good: certificate produced, number matches. Red flag: expired or borrowed documents. |
| 7 | "Do you have a TIN? Are your tax filings current?" | Needed for Paystack onboarding, invoices and the agreement. | Good: TIN letter produced. Red flag: "the accountant handles it, don't worry". |
| 8 | "Are you VAT registered, and who accounts for VAT on the room rate?" | Determines whether our all-in displayed price is actually all-in. | Good: accountant can confirm in writing. Red flag: guesswork. |
| 9 | "Is the bank account for payouts in the **business** name?" | Paystack subaccounts require matching names. | Good: account name matches CAC. Red flag: personal account for a company. |
| 10 | "Is any other entity or person to receive money from these bookings?" | Prevents hidden diversion and confusion at payout. | Good: "no". Red flag: offshore or third-party account. |

## 3. Right to operate the property

| # | Ask | Why it matters | Good answer / red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | "Which Plateau State and local government permits do you hold for hotel or short-stay operation?" | We must not list a property that cannot legally host guests. | Good: permits sighted, dates noted. Red flag: "nobody asks for those here". |
| 12 | "Do you have a current fire safety certificate and environmental health or sanitation clearance?" | Guest safety and our reputation. Record only what was actually sighted. | Good: documents produced. Red flag: promises to "get it soon". |
| 13 | "Do you hold a liquor licence, if you operate a bar?" | Separate regulatory requirement. | Good: licence or clear "no bar". |
| 14 | "Does your lease or land use permit hotel or short-let use?" | A landlord dispute can close the hotel overnight. | Good: lease shown / written landlord consent. Red flag: "the landlord knows". |
| 15 | "Are any permits up for renewal or under investigation?" | A badge must not outlive the hotel's right to operate. | Good: renewal date recorded. |

---

## 4. Right to list — contracts and conflicts

| # | Ask | Why it matters | Good answer / red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | "Do you have contracts with Booking.com, Airbnb, travel agents or other platforms? Any exclusivity clause?" | We must not breach their agreements or promise inventory we cannot deliver. | Good: contracts shown and reviewed. Red flag: "exclusivity? I don't remember". |
| 17 | "Are you bound by any non-compete, rate-parity or 'lowest price' clause?" | Could make our all-in price display impossible. | Good: clause disclosed. Red flag: hidden after launch. |
| 18 | "Can you sign the Hotel Partner Agreement giving JosStay authority to list the property and create a Paystack subaccount?" | This is the legal basis for commission and payouts. | Good: yes, after review. Red flag: "let's just start and paper it later". |
| 19 | "Can you grant JosStay a licence to use the hotel's photos, name and logo on the site, social media and ads?" | We publish and transform images. Need written permission. | Good: licence signed. Red flag: "use any photo you find". |
| 20 | "Has any guest, photographer or former agent made a claim against the hotel?" | Surfaces liabilities before we attach our name. | Good: disclosed. Red flag: concealed. |

## 5. Safety, complaints and dispute history

| # | Ask | Why it matters | Good answer / red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | "Have there been FCCPC complaints, court cases or judgments against the hotel?" | Regulators and guests expect honest dealing. | Good: disclosed with outcome. Red flag: "never, honestly" when records say otherwise. |
| 22 | "Any fire, injury, theft or security incident in the last two years?" | Affects the verification report and what we can claim. | Good: disclosed, corrective action described. |
| 23 | "Do you carry public liability or other insurance?" | Relevant to risk allocation in the agreement. | Good: policy sighted. Red flag: none and no plan. |
| 24 | "How do you handle a guest data request or privacy complaint?" | NDPA obligations apply to both of us. | Good: a named person and a process. Red flag: "we don't keep records". |

---

## 6. Photos, marks and data

| # | Ask | Why it matters | Good answer / red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | "Who took the listing photos, and do you own the rights?" | Prevents copyright claims. | Good: photographer credited and rights confirmed. Red flag: downloaded from Google. |
| 26 | "Are any guests or staff identifiable in the photos? Do you have their consent?" | Portrait and privacy risk. | Good: consent or no identifiable faces. |
| 27 | "Do you have a logo or brand mark you want us to use? Do you own it?" | Consistent, lawful use. | Good: files supplied with permission. |
| 28 | "Will you share a guest's phone number with us only for a confirmed booking?" | Our rule: hotel phone is private until booking; guest data must be handled lawfully. | Good: yes, restricted to fulfilment. Red flag: wants guest lists for marketing. |

---

## 9. Stop conditions

Do not onboard if the hotel:

- refuses to show registration or operating permits;
- wants a personal bank account for a registered company;
- asks you to hide the price, the rules or the cancellation deadline;
- refuses an agent inspection or photo licence;
- has an unresolved ownership or partnership dispute;
- insists on cash or off-platform payment to avoid commission;
- asks JosStay to guarantee occupancy or hotel service quality.

## How to use this in the meeting

These questions decide **whether the hotel is honestly presentable** and whether our agent can verify it. We are not testing their character — we are collecting facts that will appear on a public page. If a fact cannot be checked, it does not go on the page.

> Verification means: listing is real, photos match the property, pin is confirmed and power is tested. It does **not** guarantee hotel service. Say this out loud in the meeting.

---

## 1. Location and finding the place

| # | Ask | What you need to write down |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "What is the exact address, and what landmark would a first-time guest recognise?" | Street, area, landmark, nearest major road. |
| 2 | "Can you send a live location pin now?" | Lat/lng or a map link. Our agent confirms the pin on site. |
| 3 | "How far is it from the University of Jos, the city centre, Terminus and the airport?" | Distances in km or minutes. Used for "near" searches. |
| 4 | "Is the road motorable in the rainy season, and is there a signboard?" | Arrival experience. A guest who cannot find the hotel will call us. |
| 5 | "Which area should JosStay list this under?" | Rayfield, Lamingo, Little Rayfield, Bukuru, Bodija, Jos City Centre, Terminus, Shere Hills or a new area. New areas are created from hotel data. |

---

## 2. Rooms and inventory

| # | Ask | What you need to write down |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | "How many rooms do you have, and what are the room types?" | Room names, count per type, bed size, ensuite or shared, how many guests each sleeps. |
| 7 | "How many rooms can you actually commit to JosStay on a normal week and on a busy weekend?" | Real committed inventory, not the whole property if they hold rooms back. |
| 8 | "Do rooms differ in a way a guest must know before paying?" | Noise, stairs, shared bathroom, no window, smaller bed. Honest disclosure beats complaints. |
| 9 | "What is your housekeeping routine, and how often are linens changed?" | Operational fact, not a luxury claim. |
| 10 | "Do you have accessible rooms or ground-floor rooms?" | Only claim accessibility if genuinely true. |

## 3. Power, water, security, connectivity

These are the facts our badge is built on. Do not accept "good power" as an answer.

| # | Ask | What you need to write down |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | "Where does power come from — public supply, generator, solar, inverter?" | Source and capacity. |
| 12 | "Is power available 24/7? If not, what hours can you guarantee?" | Actual hours. Agent tests this during inspection. |
| 13 | "What happens at 2am if the generator fails? Who responds, and how fast?" | Named role and realistic response time. |
| 14 | "Is there running water all day? Is there a borehole or storage tank?" | Source and reliability. |
| 15 | "What security is on site — guards, gates, cameras, night reception?" | What was seen, not what is assumed. |
| 16 | "Is Wi-Fi available, in which areas, and is there a data cap?" | Areas covered and limits. |
| 17 | "Is parking available, free, and how many cars fit?" | Count and any charge. |

Only structured, checked facts go into search filters (24/7 power, water, parking, Wi-Fi, security).

## 4. Guest experience — grounded facts only

| # | Ask | What you need to write down |
|---|---|---|
| 18 | "Who usually stays with you, and why do they choose this place?" | Useful for "best for you" reasons, but only if it is a stable fact. |
| 19 | "What are the three most common questions guests ask you?" | These become honest FAQ content and train the AI assistant. |
| 20 | "What are the most common complaints, and what did you change?" | Honest operations insight. Do not publish complaints as testimonials. |
| 21 | "Is it quiet at night? Any bar, event hall, mosque, church or road nearby?" | Needed for the "quiet" search filter. |
| 22 | "What languages do staff speak?" | Useful for guest support. |
| 23 | "Can guests check in late at night? Is there a night receptionist?" | Fulfilment detail. |
| 24 | "Do you have real reviews or ratings you can show us, with permission to quote?" | If yes, we link to the source. If no, we show **nothing** — no fake stars or counts. |

## 5. Inspection logistics

| # | Ask | What you need to write down |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | "Can our agent visit today or tomorrow? What time is best?" | Book the physical visit in the meeting. |
| 26 | "Who will accompany the agent, and can they see the rooms, meter or generator and gate?" | Access is non-negotiable. |
| 27 | "Can the agent compare your current photos with the actual rooms?" | Photos-match check. |
| 28 | "Can the agent test power, confirm the pin and take fresh photos?" | The three checks the badge depends on. |
| 29 | "Are the photos you will give us current? When were they taken?" | Date-stamp them. |

The agent records a reference, not a public name. Report status is `Pass`, `Pass with notes` or `Fail`. Only the first two publish. Re-inspection is due every 12 months or after a material complaint.

## 6. Fit with the JosStay model

| # | Ask | What you need to write down |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | "Are you comfortable that guests see your name, price and rules, but not your phone number until after they book?" | Confirm acceptance of the privacy rule. |
| 31 | "Are you comfortable that JosStay is the guest's single point of contact until booking is confirmed?" | Confirms we stay the intermediary. |
| 32 | "How quickly can you respond if our support team messages you about a confirmed guest?" | Response expectation, e.g. within 30 minutes. |
| 33 | "Who is our operations contact, and what are their hours?" | Internal only. |
| 34 | "Can you block dates in a dashboard if a walk-in guest takes the last room?" | Prevents double-booking. |
| 35 | "What would make you stop working with JosStay?" | Surfaces deal-breakers early. |

---

## 7. Red flags in a non-legal meeting

- Refuses a physical inspection or wants to choose what the agent sees.
- Gives different prices depending on who is asking.
- Claims "24/7 power" but cannot say what happens when supply fails.
- Cannot produce a location pin or a clear address.
- Wants to advertise rooms that do not exist or are under construction.
- Insists on fake reviews, fake ratings or invented "verified" claims.
- Says the rules can be changed for a guest quietly after payment.

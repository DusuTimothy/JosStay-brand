## How to use this in the meeting

The hotel keeps its own rules. Our job is to make those rules **visible before payment** and keep them stable after booking. Ask the questions, write the answers exactly, and get the hotel to sign the chosen wording. Never soften a rule in the guest's face and never promise a refund the template does not allow.

> Say this in the meeting: *"You keep your rules. We show them clearly, with the real deadline date, before the guest pays. We will not rewrite them for a guest."*

## 1. Choose one cancellation template

| Template | Confirmed guest-facing rule | Confirmed refund behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Flexible** | Free cancellation until 24 hours before check-in | After that, first night non-refundable |
| **Moderate** | Free cancellation until 72 hours before check-in | 50% back until 24 hours before; none after |
| **Strict** | Free cancellation until 24 hours after booking | No refund after that window |

| # | Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Which of the three templates do you choose: flexible, moderate or strict?" | One template per hotel, chosen at onboarding. |
| 2 | "Do you accept the exact hours and percentages as written here?" | We need signed wording, not an approximation. |
| 3 | "What time zone should the deadline use?" | Nigeria local time, WAT. Write it down. |
| 4 | "Can we show the actual deadline date and time next to your rule on the hotel page and at checkout?" | A rule without a date is not useful to a guest. |
| 5 | "What counts as a cancellation — a message, a click, a phone call?" | Needed for support and evidence. |
| 6 | "If a guest arrives late or does not show up, which rule applies?" | No-shows are a separate, common case. |

If the hotel wants a custom rule, stop. The answer is: choose one of the three. Custom terms go to your sign-off, not into the product.

## 2. Check-in, check-out and arrival

| # | Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | "What time is check-in and check-out?" | Shown on the page and in the confirmation. |
| 8 | "Can a guest check in early or check out late? Is there a charge?" | Must be disclosed before payment. |
| 9 | "Is there a night reception, or should a late guest call ahead?" | Fulfilment detail released after booking. |
| 10 | "What ID do you require at check-in?" | Guests need to know in advance. |
| 11 | "Can a guest extend a stay at the same rate?" | Avoids a price surprise. |

---

## 3. Hotel-initiated cancellation — ask this explicitly

| # | Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | "If you cannot provide the booked room, will you agree to a full refund initiated immediately? Do you accept we tell guests the money usually appears in 3–10 business days?" | This is the guest's protection when the hotel fails, not the guest. Paystack confirmed the timeline on 17 Nov 2026. |
| 13 | "If we have a comparable verified hotel available, will you authorise us to move the guest at no extra cost?" | We will **not** promise re-housing publicly unless we can actually deliver it. |
| 14 | "How much notice will you give us if a room becomes unavailable?" | Lets support act before arrival. |
| 15 | "Who decides, and who signs this commitment?" | Makes it enforceable. |

Default public language: *"If the hotel cancels a confirmed booking, a full refund is initiated. We will help find an alternative where we can."* Do not add a guarantee you have not agreed with a real second hotel.

## 4. House rules the guest must see before paying

| # | Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | "What are your smoking rules, indoors and outdoors?" | Common source of disputes. |
| 17 | "Are pets allowed?" | Yes or no, in writing. |
| 18 | "Are children allowed? Any age rules or extra-bed rules?" | Affects guest count and price. |
| 19 | "Are visitors allowed in rooms? Any curfew?" | Security and conduct. |
| 20 | "Are parties, events or alcohol allowed?" | Noise and safety. |
| 21 | "What are your quiet hours?" | Needed for our "quiet" search claim. |
| 22 | "Any rules about footwear, dress or conduct in public areas?" | If they matter, show them. |
| 23 | "Do you have a damage or loss policy, including lost keys?" | Must be visible before payment. |
| 24 | "Anything else a guest regularly gets surprised by?" | Best question in the meeting. Write the answer down. |

## 5. Availability and blocks

| # | Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | "How far ahead can a guest book?" | Booking window. |
| 26 | "Who can block dates, and how quickly will you tell us?" | Prevents double-booking with walk-ins. |
| 27 | "Do you ever oversell rooms? What is your rule if you do?" | Our availability engine must be honest. |
| 28 | "If a guest needs a different room on arrival, who decides?" | Clear ownership of the decision. |

## 6. Policy changes and snapshots

| # | Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 29 | "If you change a price or rule later, how much notice will you give JosStay?" | Lets us update the page honestly. |
| 30 | "Do you accept that a confirmed booking keeps the price and policy shown at the time of booking?" | Stored snapshot; we do not rewrite past bookings. |
| 31 | "Who signs policy changes?" | One accountable person. |
| 32 | "Can you re-confirm the rule at the next 12-month re-inspection?" | Keeps the badge and policy current. |

---

## 7. Exceptions and escalation

| # | Ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 33 | "Who at the hotel can waive a fee, and within what limit?" | Guests need a fast answer without a policy breach. Any waiver must be logged. |
| 34 | "If a guest complains, what is your response target?" | We need to tell the guest when to expect an answer. |
| 35 | "Who handles an emergency at 2am?" | Safety and support handoff. |
| 36 | "May JosStay explain your rule in plain English on the website and in chat?" | Our AI policy explainer only repeats the signed rule. It cannot invent exceptions. |

---

## 8. Red flags on policy

- Wants different rules for different guests.
- Says "we'll decide when they arrive" about a fee or deposit.
- Refuses to put the chosen template in writing.
- Wants us to display "free cancellation" without a deadline.
- Changes a confirmed guest's rule after payment.
- Cannot say what happens if the hotel itself cancels.
- Asks AI or support to promise a refund outside the template.
